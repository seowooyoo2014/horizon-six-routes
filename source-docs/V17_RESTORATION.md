# Horizon Ledger V17

V17 keeps the six V16 campaigns while restoring the 960x540 V15 town, sea,
battle, duel, ship, and character presentation. The V16 half-resolution art
adapter remains in the repository but is no longer loaded by `Web/index.html`.

V17 adds six renamed selectable captains, a 3x2 original portrait sheet,
versioned save migration, 1,800 deterministic multi-room interior floors,
fourteen culture palettes, accessible stairs and hotspots, and an indoor party
trail for up to three companions. V16 and V15 save keys are preserved.

The attached classic RPG screenshots were used only as references for room
readability, stairs, and furnishing density. No source pixels are included.
